Rynex

Never lose a customer to a missed call.

Rynex sends a callback text when a small business can't pick up, so callers can follow up by text instead of calling the shop down the street.

How it works

When a call to a participating number goes unanswered, the caller hears a short message offering one text. If the caller presses 1, Rynex sends a single text message with the business's name so they can follow up. If they don't press 1, nothing is sent.

Built for small local businesses

Salons, barbershops, repair shops, and other independent businesses that can't answer every call. Each business sets the wording of its own text.
