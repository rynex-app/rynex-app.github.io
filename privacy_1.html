Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy describes how Juwon Eom, a sole proprietor in Ann Arbor, Michigan, operating as Rynex ("Rynex," "we"), handles information in its missed-call text messaging program.

Information we collect

When a call to a participating business number goes unanswered, we collect the caller's phone number, the time of the call, the business that was called, whether the caller pressed 1 to request a text message, and the delivery status of any text we send. If the caller replies, we also collect the content of that reply.

How we use it

We use this information only to send the single text message the caller requested about their missed call, to let the participating business follow up with that caller, to measure whether our service is working, and to honor opt-out requests.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. We use Twilio, a messaging provider, only to deliver messages, and we share the caller's phone number with the participating business that the caller tried to reach so that business can respond.

Message frequency and costs

You receive one text message for each missed call on which you press 1. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. You can also email juwoneom78@gmail.com.

Retention

We keep call and message records for up to 12 months and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your records sooner by emailing the address below.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
