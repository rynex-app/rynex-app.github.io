How callers opt in

Rynex, operated by Juwon Eom, sends a text only after the caller agrees to it during the call. This page shows the exact consent script and the exact messages.

Step 1: The caller hears this script

When a call to a participating number goes unanswered, an automated voice plays this script before any text is sent:

Sorry, [the business's name] missed your call. To receive one text message so you can follow up by text, press 1. By pressing 1, you agree to receive that message from Rynex and accept our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy, linked in the text. You will receive one message per missed call. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

In the live script, the business's own name is spoken at the start, for example "Sorry, Maple Street Barbers missed your call."

Step 2: The caller chooses

  • If the caller presses 1, that is express consent. One text is sent.
  • If the caller presses any other key or does nothing, the call ends and no text is sent.

Step 3: The text the caller receives

Maple Street Barbers: Hi! We missed your call. Text back and we'll get you scheduled. Sent via Rynex. Terms and Privacy: this site. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Step 4: Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to stop all messages. You will receive one confirmation and nothing further. Reply HELP for support, or email juwoneom78@gmail.com.
