Program name and operator

Rynex Missed Call Text-Back ("Rynex") is operated by Juwon Eom, a sole proprietor in Ann Arbor, Michigan, operating as Rynex.

Program description

When a call to a participating business number is not answered, the caller hears an automated message offering to send one text message so they can follow up by text. A text is sent only if the caller presses 1 on their keypad. Pressing 1 is your consent to receive that message. The message names the business that was called. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Message frequency

One message per missed call on which you press 1. If you reply, you may receive a reply from the business you called.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to cancel. After you reply STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and no further messages from this program. Reply HELP for help, or email juwoneom78@gmail.com.
